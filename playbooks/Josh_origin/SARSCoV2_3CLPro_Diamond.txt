# SARS-CoV-2 3CL Protease (Mpro) Activity Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability of biochemical activity against SARS-CoV-2 Mpro. Scoring is heuristic based on covalent warheads, peptidomimetic features, and drug-likeness.

## Scoring (Base = 2)

### A) Covalent warhead evidence
- Nitrile: +28; Aldehyde: +35; Alpha-ketoamide: +25
- Michael acceptors (acrylamide/enone): +18; Chloroacetamide: +22; Vinyl sulfone: +18
- Warhead + gamma-lactam synergy bonus: +15

### B) S1 (Gln-mimic) evidence
- Gamma-lactam/2-pyrrolidinone: +18
- Else triazole/imidazole/indazole: +8

### C) Backbone (peptidomimetic)
- Amide bonds ≥2: +10; ≥3: additional +5
- Amide bonds=0 AND no heteroaromatic: -10

### D) S2 hydrophobe plausibility
- logP 1.5-4.0: +6
- Largest ring system ≥2 AND not PAH: +3
- PAH-like: -10

### E) Drug-likeness
- MW 250-650: +4 else -6
- TPSA 50-140: +4; >170: -10
- HBA 4-11 AND HBD 0-3: +3 else -4
- |charge| <2: +2 else -12
- QED ≥0.35: +2 else -3
- Poorly soluble: -6

### F) Alert penalties
- Epoxide/aziridine: -12; Nitroso: -10; Aryl nitro: -6

### Probability mapping
Use logistic: P = 100 / (1 + exp(-(score/12 - 0.5)))
Or table: ≤-10→0-5%, -9 to 0→5-15%, 1-10→15-35%, 11-25→35-65%, 26-45→65-85%, 46-60→85-95%, >60→95-99%

### Overrides
- Warhead + gamma-lactam + amides≥2 + logP 1-4: P = max(P, 85)
- MW<180 + no warhead + no rings: P = min(P, 10)
- |charge|≥2 + TPSA>180: P = min(P, 15)
