# Skin Sensitization Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability of skin sensitization (allergic contact dermatitis). Sensitizers need (A) electrophilic or activatable motif for covalent protein binding AND (B) sufficient skin bioavailability (logP/permeability, low permanent charge).

## Key reactivity domains
1) Michael acceptors; 2) SN2 electrophiles (epoxides, aziridines, alkyl halides); 3) SNAr (activated aryl halides); 4) Acyl transfer (isocyanates, anhydrides, acyl halides); 5) Schiff base formers (aldehydes); 6) Pre/pro-haptens (catechols, anilines → quinones)

## Scoring (Base P0 = 20, ~20% prevalence)

### AlertScore (max of matched domain, 0-70)
Very high (+60-70): multi-activated SNAr, benzylic Br/I, isocyanate/isothiocyanate, quinone/quinone-imine, organic peroxides
High (+40-55): Michael acceptor with extra EWGs, epoxide/aziridine, anhydride/acyl halide/sulfonyl chloride
Moderate (+20-35): aldehyde (especially aliphatic), alpha-dicarbonyl, activated alkyl halide
Low (+5-15): pre/pro-hapten (catechol/hydroquinone/aniline), broad catalog alerts

### PhyschemAdj (-40 to +10)
- Estimate logKp = -2.72 + 0.71*logD(pH5.5) - 0.0061*MW
- logKp <-5: -30; -5 to -3: -10
- logD(pH5.5) >7: -10
- Net charge at pH 5.5: ±1 → -10; ±2 → -20
- TPSA >120 or HBD ≥3: -5

### Special cases
- Aromatic aldehydes: lower concern than aliphatic (resonance deactivation)
- Cross-linkers (multiple aldehydes/bifunctional): +10 to +20 extra
- Don't downweight reactive acylators for hydrolytic instability

### P = clamp(P0 + AlertScore + PhyschemAdj, 0, 100)
