# Human Intestinal Absorption Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Likelihood of high human intestinal absorption (fraction absorbed), primarily passive GI absorption.

## Scoring (anchored to TPSA — strongest single predictor)

### Base score from TPSA
- ≤60: 90; 60-90: 75; 90-120: 55; 120-140: 35; 140-154: 20; 154-200: 10; >200: 3

### Lipophilicity adjustment (WlogP)
- <0: -20; 0-1: -10; 1-3: +5; 3-5: +0; 5-6: -5; >6: -15

### MW adjustment
- <255: +5; 255-500: +0; 500-580: -10; >580: -20

### Flexibility (rotatable bonds)
- ≤5: +5; 6-10: +0; 11-15: -10; >15: -20

### H-bond penalties
- HBD >5: -10; >8: -20
- HBA >10: -10; >15: -20
- HBD+HBA >12: -10

### Ionization/charge at pH 7.4
- Neutral: +5; +1 (base): +0; -1 (acid): -10; zwitterion: -15; |charge| ≥2: -25; ambiguous: -5

### Hard-rule caps
- Quaternary ammonium present: cap at 15
- Bisphosphonate motif: cap at 10
- TPSA >200: cap at 10

Clamp to [0, 100].
