# hERG Blocker Assessment (IC50 < 10 µM)

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability the molecule blocks hERG (IC50 < 10 µM). Key determinants: lipophilicity increases risk (saturates ~logP 4), basic/cationic center increases risk (cation-π interactions with Y652/F656), higher polarity/TPSA decreases risk, strong acids/anions reduce risk, aromatic/hydrophobic ring content co-occurs with block.

## Scoring — choose baseline bucket (P0)

### 1) Very low (P0=5)
- logP <1.0 AND no positive states at pH 7.4 AND TPSA ≥60

### 2) Low (P0=15)
- logP <2.0 AND charge ≤0 AND TPSA ≥40

### 3) Medium (P0=40)
- logP in [2, 3) OR ionization is ambiguous

### 4) High (P0=70)
- has_positive_states AND logP ≥3.0 AND TPSA ≤90

### 5) Very high (P0=85)
- has_positive_states AND logP ≥4.0 AND TPSA ≤75 AND MW in [250, 550]

## Adjustments (apply to P0)

### Ionization
- charge ≤ -1: -25
- charge == +1: +10
- charge ≥ +2: -10 (permeability limitation)
- is_ambiguous: move 10 points toward 50

### Polarity
- TPSA ≥120: -20
- TPSA ≤40: +10

### Size / flexibility
- MW <250: -15
- MW 300-500: +5
- Rotatable bonds ≥8: +5

### Aromatic / hydrophobe topology
- aromatic_rings ≥3: +5
- PAH-like (≥3 fused aromatics): +10
- fraction_csp3 ≥0.6 AND aromatic_rings ≤1: -5

### Mitigating polar motifs
- amide_bonds ≥2: -5
- Strong anion motif AND logP <3: -10

### Classic blocker motif boost
- Tertiary/quaternary amine + aromatic_rings ≥2: +10

## Final calibration guards
- P ≥95 only if: positive states AND logP≥4 AND TPSA≤60 AND (aromatic≥2 OR PAH-like) AND not anionic
- P ≤5 only if: no positive states AND logP≤1.5 AND TPSA≥80 AND MW≤350
- If many conflicting factors (cationic but very high TPSA): keep P near 50

Clamp to [0, 100].
