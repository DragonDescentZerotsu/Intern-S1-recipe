# Oral Bioavailability Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability the molecule is orally bioavailable (viable property space for absorption). Most reliable for Ro5-ish small molecules. Less reliable for macrocycles, peptides, active-transporter substrates.

## Scoring (Base = 40)

### A) Rule-of-5 block
- 0 Lipinski violations: +20
- 1 violation: +10
- 2 violations: -10
- 3-4 violations: -25
- MW >600: additional -10

### B) TPSA block
- ≤60: +20; 60-90: +10; 90-120: +0; 120-140: -10; >140: -25

### C) Flexibility (rotatable bonds)
- ≤6: +10; 7-10: +0; 11-15: -10; >15: -20

### D) Ionization/charge at pH 7.4
- Neutral: +5; +1 (amine): +5; -1 (acid): +0; |charge| ≥2: -20; ambiguous: -5
- Quaternary ammonium or sulfonate/phosphate present: -30

### E) Solubility (ESOL logS)
- >-3: +5; -3 to -4: +0; <-4: -10; <-6: -20

### F) Aromaticity/fused aromatics
- PAH-like: -10; aromatic_rings ≥4 + fraction_csp3 <0.2: -5

### G) Structural alerts
- Alert count ≥3: -5; ≥6: -10

## Exception handling
- Macrocycles: reduce penalty severity by ~50%, avoid extremes
- Transporter substrates can violate Ro5: if violations present but resembles known transporter substrates, output 20-40 not near 0
- If macrocycle/bRo5/ambiguous: compress toward 50: P = 50 + 0.7*(P-50)

Clamp to [0, 100].
