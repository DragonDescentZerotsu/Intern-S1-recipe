# CYP3A4 Substrate Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability the molecule is a CYP3A4 substrate. CYP3A4 has a large, flexible, lipophilic binding cavity accepting diverse/large ligands. Productive binding typically involves one key polar H-bond anchor plus hydrophobic/π contacts.

## Scoring (Base S = 50, uninformed prior)

### Size / lipophilicity / flexibility
- MW: <200 -15; 200-350 +0; 350-650 +10; >650 +5
- cLogP: <1 -15; 1-2 -5; 2-5 +10; 5-7 +5; >7 -10
- RotB: 0-2 -5; 3-8 +5; >8 +0
- Aromatic rings: 0 -10; 1-3 +5; ≥4 +0

### Polar anchor / pharmacophore
- HBA: 0 -25; 1-6 +10; 7-10 +5; >10 -10
- HBD: 0-2 +5; 3-4 +0; >4 -10
- TPSA: <20 -5; 20-90 +10; 90-140 +0; >140 -20
- Pharmacophore: Acceptors≥2 AND Donors≥1 AND Hydrophobes: +5

### Ionization/charge (pH 7.4)
- Net charge 0: +5; +1: +3; ≤-1: -8; |charge| ≥2 or quaternary ammonium: -20; zwitterion: -8

### Red flags
- Permanent anions (sulfonate/phosphonate): -25
- PAH-like (≥3 fused aromatics): -10
- Heteroatoms >12: -10

### Heme-coordination motif (ambiguous)
- Imidazole/triazole/pyridine + cLogP ≥2: +3

### Interpretation
- 80-100: MW 300-700, cLogP 2-6, TPSA 20-90, RotB 3-8, neutral/+1, has anchor+hydrophobe
- 40-79: mixed signals
- 0-39: very polar, permanently charged, poly-anionic, or lacks heteroatoms

Clamp to [0, 100].
