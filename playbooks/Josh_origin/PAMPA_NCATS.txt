# PAMPA Permeability Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability of high passive membrane permeability (PAMPA). This is a purely passive diffusion assay — no transporters.

## Scoring (Base score = 50)

### Unionized fraction (pKa-derived, largest driver)
- fraction_neutral ≥0.30: +20; 0.10-0.30: +10; 0.03-0.10: +0; 0.01-0.03: -10; <0.01: -20

### Charge-state penalties
- |net charge| ≥2: -30
- Zwitterion: -25
- Ambiguous: -10
- LogD warnings (polyprotic/amphoteric): -5

### Delocalized cation exception
- Quaternary ammonium BUT aromatic_rings ≥2: add back +10

### Lipophilicity (logD7.4)
- 2.0-4.0: +15; 1.0-2.0: +5; 4.0-5.0: +0; <1.0: -10; >5.0: -10

### Polarity (TPSA)
- <60: +15; 60-90: +5; 90-120: -10; >120: -25

### H-bonding
HBD: 0-1 +10; 2 +5; 3 -5; ≥4 -15
HBA: ≤6 +5; 7-10 +0; >10 -10

### Size/flexibility
MW: <350 +10; 350-500 +0; 500-700 -10; >700 -25
RB: ≤5 +5; 6-10 +0; >10 -10

### Macrocycle adjustments
- If macrocycle: soften MW penalty +10
- If macrocycle + high amide count > HBD (N-methylation proxy): +10

### Hard stops
- ≥2 carboxylic acids OR sulfonic/phosphonic acid: -20
- Guanidinium: -25

Clamp to [0, 100].
