# Clinical Toxicity Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
P(fail clinical trials due to toxicity). Safety/toxicity accounts for ~20-30% of clinical-stage failures. Start most drug-like structures at ~20-30 and adjust.

## Scoring (Base P = 25)

### A) hERG/QT risk
- Basic at pH 7.4 AND logD7.4 >1.4: +15
- Basic AND logD7.4 ≤1.4: +5
- Neutral AND logD7.4 >3.3: +10
- Aromatic rings ≥3: +5
- TPSA ≤60: +5

### B) DILI/reactive metabolite
- Each high-value RM alert (aniline, nitroaromatic, phenol/catechol, thiophene, furan, quinone): +6 each (cap +24)
- logP ≥3: +8; logP ≥4.5: +12 (replaces +8)

### C) Direct electrophile/genotox
- Epoxide or aziridine: +25
- Acyl halide / sulfonyl chloride / isocyanate: +25
- Activated alkyl halide or strong Michael acceptor: +15

### D) Phospholipidosis/CAD
- Most basic pKa ≥7.5 AND logP ≥3 AND ≥1 aromatic ring: +10
- ≥2 basic sites: +5

### E) Complexity/exposure modifiers
- ESOL poorly soluble: +5
- MW ≥550: +5
- Zwitterionic or strongly acidic + logP <2: -5

### Key principle
Convergent evidence (alert + enabling properties) is much stronger than either alone. Don't treat any single alert as deterministic.

Clamp to [0, 100].
