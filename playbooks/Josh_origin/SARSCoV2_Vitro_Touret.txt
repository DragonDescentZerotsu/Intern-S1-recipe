# Anti-SARS-CoV-2 In Vitro Activity Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
P(molecule shows measurable anti-SARS-CoV-2 activity). Many reported "hits" are artifacts. This playbook boosts well-precedented antiviral chemotypes and penalizes PAINS/aggregation/lysosomotropic patterns.

## Scoring (Base P = 2)

### Property fit (0-20)
- MW 250-600: +6; cLogP 1-4: +6; TPSA 40-120: +4; RotB ≤10: +2; QED ≥0.35: +2

### Mpro warhead boost (0-30)
- Nitrile + peptidomimetic (≥2 amides, MW>350): +18
- Terminal alkyne + peptidomimetic: +10
- Alpha-ketoamide + peptidomimetic: +14
- Michael acceptor in binding scaffold: +8
- Alpha-haloacetamide in scaffold: +10

### Protease scaffold boost (0-20)
- Amide bonds ≥2: +6; ≥3: +10; Lactam: +6; CF3: +2-4

### RdRp nucleoside boost (0-25)
- Nucleoside-like core (MW 200-550, high TPSA, multiple O, sugar-like ring): +12
- Prodrug esters: +6; Heteroaromatic base: +4

### TMPRSS2 boost (0-20)
- Guanidinium/amidinium on aromatic: +10; Ester: +4; Positive states: +2

### Use max of (MproBoost, RdRpBoost, TMPRSS2Boost)

### Artifact/nonspecific penalties
- PAINS ≥1: -15 (multiple: -25); Many cross-library alerts: -25 to -40
- Selenium/disulfide: -20; Quinones/catechols: -10 to -25
- Aggregation risk (logP>4.5 + aromatic≥3 + TPSA<60): -15; PAH-like: -10

### Lysosomotropic penalty (nonspecific in vitro)
- Positive states + logP≥3 + TPSA≤60-70 + aromatic≥2: -25 to -30
- 3/4 criteria: -15

Clamp to [0, 100].
