# CYP2C9 Substrate Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability the molecule is metabolized by CYP2C9. Substrates are often aromatic, lipophilic, neutral to weakly acidic, frequently containing an anionizable group and H-bonding features.

## Scoring (Base score = 15)

### A) Ionization / acidic character (largest signal)
- has_negative_states: +25
- Representative net_charge == -1: additional +10
- has_positive_states AND no negative: -20
- is_ambiguous: -5

### B) Acidic/anionizable motifs
- Carboxylic acid: +20; Tetrazole: +18; Sulfonylurea-like: +15; Imide-like: +10; Phenol (sole acidity): +5

### C) Aromaticity
- 1-3 aromatic rings: +12; 0 aromatic rings: -10; PAH-like: -5

### D) Lipophilicity
- logP 2-5: +10; logP <1: -10; logP >6: -5

### E) Size & polarity
- MW 200-500: +8; MW <150: -8; MW >600: -15
- TPSA 20-90: +6; TPSA >120: -15

### F) H-bond donors / flexibility
- HBD 0-2: +4; HBD ≥4: -10
- Rotatable bonds 2-8: +3; >12: -8

### G) Inhibitor-like heme ligators
- Imidazole/triazole: -15; Pyridine (no acidity): -8

### Applicability domain
- No aromatics AND TPSA >120 AND logP <1: force prob ≤20
- Permanently charged (|charge| ≥2): cap ≤30

### Typical CYP2C9 substrate patterns
- Aryl-(CH2/CH)-CO2H (NSAID-like); Diaryl + acid (diclofenac-like)
- Sulfonylurea with para-substituted phenyl (tolbutamide-like)
- Hydantoin/imide with phenyl rings (phenytoin-like)

Clamp to [0, 100].
