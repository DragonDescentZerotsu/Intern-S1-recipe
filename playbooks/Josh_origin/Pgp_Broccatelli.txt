# P-glycoprotein Inhibition Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability the molecule inhibits P-gp (ABCB1). Inhibitors are often hydrophobic, aromatic, contain a basic/tertiary nitrogen, with H-bonding functionality. Many inhibitors are also substrates.

## Scoring (Base P = 25)

### A) Size/volume
- MW <250: -25; 250-350: +0; 350-500: +10; ≥500: +15

### B) Hydrophobicity (logP)
- <1.5: -20; 1.5-3.0: +0; 3.0-4.5: +12; 4.5-6.5: +10; >6.5: -5

### C) Aromaticity/rings
- 0 aromatic: -15; 1: +5; ≥2: +12; PAH-like: +5

### D) Ionization/basicity
- has_positive_states: +12
- Tertiary amine / heterocyclic amine motif: +8 (cap basicity total at +18)
- has_negative_states AND no positive: -15
- Strong acid (carboxylate/sulfonate at pH 7.4): -10 extra

### E) H-bonding
- HBA ≥2: +5; HBD ≥1: +3
- TPSA >160: -10; TPSA 40-120: +5

### F) Aryl ether/methoxy
- Aryl ether or multiple methoxy groups: +5

### G) Flexibility
- RotB ≥8: +3; >14: -5

### Interpretation bands
- 0-20: very unlikely (small, polar, anionic)
- 21-40: unlikely/unknown
- 41-60: plausible (aromatic + moderate logP + HBA)
- 61-80: likely (aromatic + hydrophobic + basic N)
- 81-100: very likely

Clamp to [0, 100].
