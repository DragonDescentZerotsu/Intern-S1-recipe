# Carcinogenicity Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability of carcinogenicity in standard rodent bioassays. Most structure-based signal comes from identifying DNA-reactive electrophiles or bioactivation precursors ("structural alerts"). Non-genotoxic carcinogens are harder to predict from structure alone.

## Scoring (Base risk = 15)

### Very high concern (+55 to +75)
- N-nitroso group (nitrosamine/nitrosamide/nitrosourea)
- Epoxide/aziridine or mustard-like bifunctional alkylator

### High concern (+30 to +50)
- Primary aromatic amine / hydroxylamine derivatives
- Aromatic nitroso / nitroaromatic
- Hydrazine, azide/triazene, acyl halide, sultone/lactone, sulfonate/phosphonate esters

### Moderate concern (+15 to +30)
- Michael acceptor (α,β-unsaturated carbonyl)
- PAH-like fused aromatics (≥3 fused rings)

### Modulators (+0 to +10)
- logP >3: may increase potency within classes
- Large fused aromatic system: additional concern

### Mitigations (-0 to -15)
- Steric hindrance near aromatic amine (bulky ortho groups)
- Highly polar/charged with very low logP (reduced uptake)
- Sulfonic acid substituents on aromatic amines

### Non-genotoxic handling
- When no genotoxic alerts fire: do NOT output 0-10
- Keep baseline 10-25 unless strong benign-neighbor evidence

### Calibration bands
- 0-10: no alerts, benign-like; 20-40: no strong genotoxic alerts; 50-70: one strong or multiple moderate; 80-95: very high-confidence electrophile; 96-100: multiple very high concern + PAH

Clamp to [0, 100].
