# AMES Mutagenicity Assessment Guide

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
AMES positivity = bacterial reverse mutation mutagenicity. Positives are enriched for:
1) DNA-reactive electrophiles (direct alkylators): epoxides, aziridines
2) Metabolically activated pro-electrophiles: aromatic amines (nitrenium pathway), nitroarenes (nitroreduction → hydroxylamine)
3) Planar polyaromatic systems with nitro/amine substituents

Important: ~22-27% of compounds with NO structural alerts are still AMES-positive. Absence of alerts does not guarantee negative.

## Scoring rubric (start P=25)

### Strong direct-acting electrophiles
- Epoxide: +40
- Aziridine: +45
- Nitrogen mustard motif: +50
- Acyl halide: +35
- Primary/benzylic alkyl halide: +25
- Multiple strong electrophiles (2+): additional +15

### High-risk pro-electrophiles
- Aromatic nitro: +35 (frequently potent bacterial mutagens)
- N-nitroso: +40
- Nitroso (non-nitrosamine): +25
- Primary aromatic amine: +25
- Azo bond: +15

### Moderate-risk reactivity
- Michael acceptor: +15
- Aldehyde: +10
- Quinone-like + aromatic system: +10

### Ring-system potentiation
- PAH-like (≥3 fused aromatic rings): +15
- If also has aromatic nitro or amine: +10 extra

### Mitigations (subtract, never below 0)
- Aromatic amine with strong EWG on same ring (CF3, sulfonyl, carboxyl): -10
- Sulfonic/sulfamic acid present: -10
- Highly ionized at pH 7.4 (|net charge| ≥2): -10
- Simple aromatic N-oxide only (no other alerts): -10

### Post-processing
- If no alerts AND drug-like (MW 150-500, logP 1-3): keep near 20-35
- If multiple independent high-risk alerts (e.g., epoxide + aromatic nitro): force P ≥ 85
- Clamp to [0, 100]
