# DILI (Drug-Induced Liver Injury) Assessment

## This guide helps guide a probability P (0-100) assessment for the task.
Probability the molecule causes human DILI. DILI risk signal comes from (i) structural alerts and (ii) physicochemical properties (notably lipophilicity and aromatic ring count). Dose/exposure dominates many cases but is unknown from SMILES.

## Scoring (Base P_raw = 20)

### Structural alert points
- Hydrazine/hydrazide: +30
- Furan: +25
- Aniline: +15
- Sulfonamide: +10
- Other alerts (alkyl halide, thioether, thiol, sulfoxide, epoxide, Michael acceptor): +8 each (cap +25 total)

### Physicochemical risk
LogP: <2 +0; 2-3 +5; 3-4 +12; ≥4 +18
Aromatic rings: 0-1 +0; 2 +6; 3 +12; ≥4 +18
MW: <300 +0; 300-399 +4; 400-499 +8; ≥500 +12
ESOL logS: >-3 +0; -4 to -3 +5; ≤-4 +10

### Ring-system liability
- PAH-like (≥3 fused aromatics): +10

### Cholestasis/BSEP proxy
- If logP ≥3 AND has_positive_states AND TPSA ≤75: +8

### Reductions
- TPSA ≥140: -8
- Zwitterion AND TPSA ≥120: -8
- Formal charge ≤-1 AND logP <2: -5

### Safety cap
- If no alerts AND logP <2.5 AND aromatic≤1 AND MW<350: cap at 35

### Uncertainty dampening
- If ambiguous ionization OR many generic alerts OR high-property-risk but no DILI alerts:
  P_final = round(50 + 0.7*(P_raw - 50))
- Else: P_final = round(P_raw)

Clamp to [0, 100].
